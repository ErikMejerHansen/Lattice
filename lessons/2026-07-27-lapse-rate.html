A parcel of air rises from the surface. Below the lifting condensation level (LCL, marked) it follows the dry adiabatic lapse of 9.8 K/km. Above the LCL, condensation releases latent heat and slows the cooling to about 5 K/km. The dashed grey line is the globally averaged environmental lapse of 6.5 K/km, the value adopted in the U.S. Standard Atmosphere. Drag T₀ and RH and watch how a warmer, wetter surface pushes the LCL down and lifts the whole parcel column into the moist regime sooner.
