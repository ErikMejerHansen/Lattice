Log-log plot of stellar luminosity against stellar mass. The rust curve is the empirical Eddington relation L ∝ M^3.5; the dashed grey line is the Eddington limit L_Edd ∝ M, above which radiation pressure would blow the star apart. Drag the mass slider to slide a star along the curve and read off its luminosity and main-sequence lifetime.
